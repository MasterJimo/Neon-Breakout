ABOUT NEON BREAKOUT

Break the bricks. Keep the ball alive. Chase your high score.

Neon Breakout is a free, single-player brick-breaking arcade game created by MrJim. Move your neon paddle to bounce the ball, destroy every brick and clear all three sectors. No account or installation is required.

How to play

Use the paddle to send the ball toward the glowing bricks. You start with 3 lives; losing the ball costs one. Clear a sector to advance to the next. Some tougher bricks need two hits, and breaking bricks in quick succession builds your combo score.

Computer controls

  • ← / →, A / D, or move your mouse left and right — move the paddle.
  • Space or left-click on the playfield — launch the ball.
  • P or Esc — pause or resume the game.
  • M — toggle sound; the speaker icon also works.

Phone and tablet controls

Drag left or right on the playfield to move your paddle, and tap to launch the ball. Use the on-screen pause and sound buttons when needed.

Power-ups

Bricks with a glowing hollow outline contain a power-up. Break them and catch the falling bonus with your paddle:

  • W — Wide Paddle: widens the paddle temporarily.
  • M — Multiball: puts extra balls into play.
  • S — Slow-Mo: slows the ball down temporarily.

Your personal best

The game remembers your best score on the same device using browser storage. There is no global leaderboard or player account. Details are explained in the Privacy & Storage page.
